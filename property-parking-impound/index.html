Parking Lot Towing

Property owners and managers use us to remove unauthorized vehicles from private lots. Pennsylvania has rules on signs and notice, so confirm yours before you request a tow.

Questions about Parking Lot Towing

Who can request a tow?
Property owners or authorized managers.
Do signs need to be posted?
Pennsylvania has rules on signage and notice. Check with the property's legal advisor.
How do I reclaim a towed car?
Call us for location and requirements.
What do I need to pick it up?
ID, proof of ownership, and payment. Call for specifics.
Can a business set up a standing agreement?
Call to discuss.

Parking Enforcement

Questions about Parking Enforcement

Who can use this service?
Property owners, managers, and businesses.

Pennsylvania and local rules apply. Property owners should confirm signage and notice.
Can you patrol regularly?
Call to discuss options.

We store it and the owner can reclaim it. Call for details.

Blocked Driveway Towing

A stranger's car parked across your driveway? We can remove it. Check local rules first, since some towns want a police report. Have proof the driveway is yours.

Can I have a car towed from my driveway?
Often yes. Rules apply. Call us.
Should I call police first?
Check local rules. Some towns want a report.
What do you need from me?
Proof the driveway is yours and the vehicle details.
Where does the car go?
To our lot.
How fast can you respond?
Call for an estimate.

Repossessions

We recover vehicles for lenders and recovery agencies. If your car was repossessed, call your lender, or a lawyer for legal questions. We can arrange access to personal belongings left inside.

Questions about Repossessions

Who hires you?
Lenders and recovery agencies.
Do you work with individuals?
Call to ask.
What are my rights if my car was repossessed?
Contact your lender or a lawyer. We are not a legal advisor.
How do I get items from a repossessed car?
Call us to arrange it.
Where is the vehicle held?
At our lot or the lender's chosen location.
