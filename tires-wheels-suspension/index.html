Tires

We sell, mount, and balance tires. Low tread, cracks, and bulges mean it is time for new ones. Bring your tire size from the sidewall.

Questions about Tires

How do I know my tires are worn out?
Low tread, cracks, or bulges.
Do I need all four?
All-wheel-drive often needs matched tires.
How do I find my tire size?
It is on the sidewall.

Tire Repair

We fix punctures and slow leaks when the damage allows. Sidewall damage usually means replacement. Do not drive on a flat.

Questions about Tire Repair

Can every puncture be fixed?
No. Sidewall damage usually means replacement.
How do I spot a slow leak?
Pressure drops in a few days.
Is a plug enough?
A proper patch from inside the tire is the better repair.
How long does it take?
Often a short visit. Call for current wait times.
Should I drive on a flat?
No. It can ruin the tire and wheel.
Related:TiresTire Rotation

Tire Rotation

Rotating tires moves each one to a new position so wear stays even. Many makers suggest every 5,000 to 7,500 miles. Check your manual.

Questions about Tire Rotation

How often should I rotate?
Many makers advise about every 5,000 to 7,500 miles. Check your manual.
Does rotation save money?
Even wear usually extends tire life.
Can I rotate if tires differ in size?
Not always. Call us about staggered setups.
Do all-wheel-drive cars need rotation?
Yes, often more regularly.

Retreads

A retread adds new tread to a sound tire casing. Retreads are common on trucks and fleets. The casing must be in good condition.

Questions about Retreads

What is a retread?
A used casing with new tread bonded on.
Are they safe?
Properly made retreads are widely used on commercial vehicles.
Do they cost less?
Often yes.
Which vehicles use them?
Mostly trucks and commercial fleets.
Can any tire be retreaded?
No. The casing must be in good condition.

Wheels

We supply, fit, and balance wheels. Size changes can affect fit, clearance, and your tire pressure sensors, so call us with your vehicle.

Questions about Wheels

Can you fit new wheels?
Yes. Call with your vehicle.
Do I need to rebalance?
Yes, after changes.
Can I switch to larger wheels?
Sometimes. Fit and clearance matter.
Do wheels affect my TPMS?
Yes. Sensors must carry over.
Related:Wheel RepairTires

Wheel Repair

Bent or curb-damaged wheels cause vibration and slow leaks. We inspect and repair what can be saved. Severe cracks mean replacement.

Questions about Wheel Repair

Can a bent wheel be fixed?
Often, yes. We inspect it first.
Can every damaged wheel be repaired?
No. Severe cracks usually mean replacement.
What does a bent wheel feel like?
Vibration at highway speed or a slow air leak.
Can I drive on a damaged wheel?
We advise against it. Damage can get worse and affect handling.
Related:WheelsTires

Ball Joints

Ball joints connect your wheels to your suspension. Clunking, wandering steering, and uneven tire wear point to wear. A failed ball joint is a safety risk, so do not wait.

Questions about Ball Joints

What are the signs of a worn ball joint?
Clunking, wandering steering, uneven tire wear.
Is a bad ball joint dangerous?
Yes. It can fail and cause loss of control.
Does it affect inspection?
Yes, it can fail inspection.
Do I need an alignment after?
Often yes.
Do they last long?
Many last years, but wear varies.
