Engine Service & Repair

We diagnose and repair engines, from tune-ups to major work. Knocking, smoke, power loss, and check engine lights are all reasons to call. When damage is large, we quote repair against replacement.

Questions about Engine Service & Repair

What are common warning signs?
Knocking, smoke, power loss, or a check engine light.
Do you do rebuilds?
Call to ask.
How do I extend engine life?
Regular oil changes and cooling system care.
Engine Service & Repair: Repair or replace?
We compare costs and tell you.
Can I drive with the check engine light on?
A steady light is less urgent than a flashing one. Flashing means stop.

Cylinder Head & Block Repair

Overheating can crack or warp cylinder heads and blocks. We inspect the damage and quote repair or replacement. Do not keep driving with a coolant leak or milky oil.

Questions about Cylinder Head & Block Repair

What causes a cracked head?
Overheating is the most common cause.
What are the warning signs?
White exhaust smoke, milky oil, and coolant loss.
Is a blown head gasket the same thing?
Not exactly. A head gasket failure can come with head damage.
Is repair worth it versus a replacement engine?
Depends on the damage and vehicle value. We quote both.
Can I keep driving with coolant loss?
No. Driving can destroy the engine.

Fuel System Service & Repair

Fuel pumps, filters, injectors, and lines all feed your engine. We diagnose hard starts, stalling, and poor power, then repair what is worn. Treat a fuel leak as urgent.

Questions about Fuel System Service & Repair

What are the signs of a fuel problem?
Hard starts, stalling, poor power, or bad mileage.
How often should the fuel filter change?
Check your manual.
Can dirty fuel hurt my engine?
Yes.
Is a fuel leak urgent?
Yes. Stop driving and call us.
Can injectors be cleaned?
Often, depending on the condition.

Gas Tanks

We repair and replace fuel tanks, straps, and lines. A fuel smell or drips under the car mean stop driving and call us, because fuel is a fire hazard.

Questions about Gas Tanks

How do I know my tank is leaking?
A fuel smell, drips under the car, or a drop in mileage.
Is a leaking tank dangerous?
Yes. Fuel is a fire hazard. Stop driving and call us.
Can tanks be repaired?
Some can. Many are replaced.
What causes tank failure?
Rust, impact damage, and age.
Will you fix the fuel pump too?
Ask about it. The pump is often inside the tank.
Related:Fuel System Service & Repair

Spark Plugs

Worn spark plugs cause misfires, rough idle, and poor mileage. We replace them at the interval your maker specifies and check related ignition parts.

Questions about Spark Plugs

When do spark plugs need changing?
Intervals vary. Check your manual.
What are signs of worn plugs?
Misfires, rough idle, and poor mileage.
Do plugs affect emissions?
Yes.
Can I change them myself?
Some are easy, others hard to reach.
Should coils be replaced too?
Sometimes. We check.

On Board Computer Specialists

Modern cars run on computers. We scan for trouble codes and diagnose electronic faults, then repair the cause instead of only clearing the light.

Questions about On Board Computer Specialists

What is a diagnostic scan?
A scan pulls trouble codes from your car's computer.
Does a code show exactly what's broken?
Not always. A code points to a system.
Can you fix warning lights?
We find the cause, then repair it.
Do you reprogram modules?
Call to ask about your vehicle.
Will a battery issue affect electronics?
Yes, often.

Alternator Installation & Repair

The alternator charges your battery while the engine runs. If your lights dim or a battery warning appears, we test the charging system and repair or replace the alternator.

Questions about Alternator Installation & Repair

What are the signs of a bad alternator?
Dim lights, a battery warning light, or dying while driving.
How do I tell it from the battery?
A test tells us. Call us.
Can I drive with a bad alternator?
Only a short way. Your car will stall when the battery drains.
Do you repair or replace?
Depends on the unit.
How long does it last?
Many last years. Age and use matter.
Related:Jump StartsBattery Recycling

Differential Repair

The differential lets your wheels turn at different speeds in corners. Whining or clunking, especially in turns, can signal wear or low fluid. We inspect, service, and repair it.

Questions about Differential Repair

What does a bad differential sound like?
Whining, howling, or clunking, especially in turns.
Does it need fluid changes?
Yes. Check your manual for intervals.
Can I keep driving with a noisy differential?
Risky. Damage can spread.
Do front and rear differentials differ?
Yes. Which you have depends on your drivetrain.
Differential Repair: What causes failure?
Low fluid, wear, and heavy loads.
Related:Axles

Axles

We repair and replace axles and CV joints. Clicking in turns, vibration, and grease leaks are common signs of wear, often from a torn boot.

Questions about Axles

Signs of a bad axle?
Clicking in turns, vibration, or grease leaks.
Can I drive with a bad CV joint?
Briefly. Failure can leave you stranded.
Axles: Repair or replace?
Often replace the axle assembly.
Axles: What causes failure?
A torn boot lets in dirt and loses grease.
Does it affect inspection?
Possibly.
Related:Differential RepairBall Joints
