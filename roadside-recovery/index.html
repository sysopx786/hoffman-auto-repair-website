Roadside Assistance

Stuck on the road? We handle towing, jump starts, lockouts, and winching. Call 484-921-0715 with your location and a description of the problem.

Questions about Roadside Assistance

What can you help with?
Towing, jump starts, lockouts, and winching. Ask for other needs.
How do I request help?
Call 484-921-0715.
Roadside Assistance: What should I do while waiting?
Move to safety, turn on hazards, and stay clear of traffic.
What do you need to know?
Your location, vehicle, and the problem.

Jump Starts

A dead battery is a common reason a car will not start. We jump the vehicle and check whether the battery or the charging system is at fault, so you do not get stranded again.

Questions about Jump Starts

Why did my battery die?
Age, cold weather, lights left on, or a bad alternator.
Will one jump fix it?
Only if the battery is healthy. Many need replacement.
How long do car batteries last?
Often three to five years.
Can you test the battery?
Ask when you call.
Should I keep the engine running?
Let it run a while, then get the charging system checked.

Winching

When a vehicle sits in a ditch, mud, or snow and cannot drive out, we winch it free using rated straps and attachment points to limit damage.

Questions about Winching

When is winching needed?
When your vehicle is stuck and can't drive out.
Will winching damage my car?
Done right, damage risk is low.
Can you winch from a ditch?
Usually.
Winching: What should I do while waiting?
Stay clear and turn on hazards.
What if the vehicle is on its side?
Call us. Recovery of rollovers needs planning.

Four Wheel Drive Vehicle Recovery

We recover 4x4 trucks and SUVs stuck in mud, snow, or off-road spots. Tell us where you are and what the ground is like, and we plan the pull.

Questions about Four Wheel Drive Vehicle Recovery

What if my truck is stuck in snow?
Stay put and call us. Spinning tires can dig you in deeper.
Will recovery damage my truck?
We use proper straps and rated points to limit risk.
Do I need tow points on my vehicle?
Many 4x4s have recovery points. Tell us what yours has.
Can you reach remote spots?
Depends on access. Give us your location.
What should I do while I wait?
Stay clear of traffic and turn on hazards.
Related:WinchingFlat Bed Truck Service

Underwater Recovery

We retrieve vehicles from ponds, creeks, and other water hazards. Call 911 first if anyone is in danger. After recovery, have the vehicle inspected before anyone tries to start the engine.

Questions about Underwater Recovery

What should I do if my car goes into water?
Get everyone out safely and call 911 first. Then call us for recovery.
Should I try to start a flooded car?
No. Starting it can destroy the engine. Let us tow it.
Will my insurance cover recovery?
Many comprehensive policies do. Check with your insurer.
Can a flooded car be saved?
Sometimes. Water type and exposure time matter. We can inspect and advise.
Do you recover vehicles in any location?
Access matters. Call us with the location and conditions.
