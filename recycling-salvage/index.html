Battery Recycling

Old car batteries hold lead and acid and do not belong in household trash. We take them and send them for recycling. Call before you drop one off.

Questions about Battery Recycling

Why can't I throw out an old battery?
Lead and acid harm soil and water. Pennsylvania law requires proper handling.
How do I carry a battery safely?
Keep it upright in a box or tray. Wear gloves.
Do you recycle other batteries?
Call us to ask about hybrid, motorcycle, or other types.

Oil Recycling

We accept used motor oil for recycling. Carry it in a sealed, labeled container and call ahead for limits.

Questions about Oil Recycling

Why recycle?
Used oil pollutes water and soil.
How should I carry it?
In a sealed, labeled container.
How much can I bring?
Call to ask.

Why not throw tires away?

Freon & Coolant Recycling

We recover refrigerant and coolant during service and send them for proper handling. Releasing refrigerant into the air is illegal, and coolant is toxic to people and pets.

Questions about Freon & Coolant Recycling

Why is refrigerant recovery required?
Releasing refrigerant into the air is illegal and harms the environment.
Why is old coolant hazardous?
It is toxic to people and pets.
How often should coolant be replaced?
Intervals vary by vehicle. Check your manual.
Do you service A/C systems?
Call to confirm A/C availability.
